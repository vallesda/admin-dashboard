## Persona Red Flags

"Por ocasión" (ceviche Saturday): taps Ceviche in "Para qué lo quieres" — the page's best moment — but lib/occasions.ts admits the admin has no concept of an occasion, so the collection cannot filter by one. The homepage promises guidance the destination does not keep.

"Recurrente" (weekly pantry): no search field, no filter, no reorder, no price sort, grid capped at 8 with no "Ver todo". Must scroll past a story about the sea to reach the catalogue — exactly what principle 4 exists to prevent.

First-time cautious buyer: every trust cue is a self-assertion. No address, phone, hours, or map. The only contact channel is href="https://instagram.com" — the generic Instagram homepage, not the shop's account. Worse than no link for a local buyer whose natural verification step is checking Instagram.

Screen-reader user: well served overall; the "Producto fresco" section (app/page.tsx:40) is the only band with no aria-labelledby — the most important section is the one unlabelled landmark.

Mobile, one-handed: hero collapses photo-first so the CTA sits well below the fold. Nine tap targets under 44px including navbar and footer links (~20px tall) and cart "Quitar" (~20px) — which is also text-muted, so it carries the contrast failure and the size failure in one control.
